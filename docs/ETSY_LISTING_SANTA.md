## Description

**Run the whole office / family gift exchange from one beautiful workbook.** 🎅

The Secret Santa & White Elephant Party Tracker from **Novality Store** is a
ready-to-use Excel template that handles the name draw, the budget rules, the
wishlists AND the white-elephant steal-fest — no macros, no formulas to write,
nothing to install.

**🎅 SECRET SANTA DRAW**
• Add participants once — the draw assigns every giver a recipient
  automatically, and *nobody can ever draw themselves* (the maths is a closed
  cycle, not a random guess).
• Exclusion rules: couples/households apart, same-team apart, no repeat of
  last year's pairing, plus your own custom no-pairs. The draw validates
  itself and flags any breach (Self / Couple / Team / LastYear / Custom).
• Change one seed number to shuffle a brand-new draw; override any single
  pair by hand; then lock the draw with the status dropdown.
• Private recipient lookup: type a name, see one recipient, pass the laptop
  on — zero spoilers.
• Printable cut-out Santa Cards so each guest discovers their recipient at
  the door.
• Per-person status: Not Started / Gift Purchased / Wrapped / Complete.

**💰 BUDGET RULES**
• Set a minimum AND maximum gift budget; every spend is flagged Under /
  Within / Over budget with colour.
• Actual spend per giver, receipt tracking with order refs, total party
  spending and average gift cost calculated live.

**🎲 WHITE ELEPHANT, FULLY RUNNABLE**
• Seat order and gift numbers shuffle from a seed; log every Pick / Steal /
  Pass on the Game History tab (works from a phone in Google Sheets).
• The game tab updates steal counts, current holders, who-stole-from-whom,
  locks gifts automatically at your max-steals limit, highlights whose turn
  it is, and records each player's final gift.

**📊 DASHBOARD** — participants, assignments done, gifts purchased, total
budget, actual spend, average gift cost, exclusion rules armed, white-elephant
players, gifts in play, locked gifts, completion %, readiness bars and live
charts.

**PLUS** — 👥 Participants with RSVP, teams, households & dietary needs •
🎁 Wishlists with priorities and anonymous claiming • 🚫 Exclusions & Rules
control centre • 🎟️ printable Santa Cards • 📖 Start Here tour with 10 pro
tips.

**TWO THEMES** — Noel Classic (holly red, pine & antique gold on cream) and
Arctic Frost (navy, ice blue & winter berry). Pick your party's mood.

**EXAMPLE FILES INCLUDED** — a fully played fictional office party (12 guests,
a locked draw, a mid-game white elephant with steals and a locked gift) so you
can see exactly how it works before touching your own copy.

**USE IT EVERY YEAR** — the party year and date are settings, not hard-coded.
Bump them each December and reuse forever: office parties, family exchanges,
school events, friendsgiving.

**WORKS IN** Excel 2016+ (Windows & Mac), Microsoft 365 and Google Sheets
(import). Formulas only — no macros, no add-ins. Formula cells are protected
(password included in the workbook's instructions) so nobody breaks the maths.

## FAQ block

**Can couples really never draw each other?** Arm the couple rule and give
them the same Household value — the draw flags any breach instantly, and a
new seed or a one-line override fixes it.

**Does the White Elephant part need macros?** No. You log turns; formulas do
the rest — holders, steal counts, locks, final gifts.

**How private is the private lookup?** It shows one name at a time, and the
printable cards keep secrets sealed until you hand them out.

**Can I reuse it next year?** Yes — change the party year, date and seeds;
keep your lists and households.
