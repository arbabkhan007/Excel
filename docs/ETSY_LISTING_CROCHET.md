## Description

**Finally know what every craft fair actually earned you.** 🧶

The Crochet Craft Fair Profit & Inventory Tracker is a ready-to-use Excel
workbook built for crochet makers who sell at markets, fairs, fetes and
online. Log a sale once — profit, margins, best-sellers, stock left and your
monthly summary all update themselves. No formulas to write, ever.

**WHAT'S INSIDE (14 tabs, Premium)**
• 📊 Profit Dashboard — total sales, net profit, margin, units sold,
  best-seller, top fair, 6 auto-updating charts and live low-stock panels
• 🧶 Product Catalog — price, yarn cost, packaging, hours, unit cost,
  profit, margin, stock and reorder status per design
• 🧵 Yarn & Materials — what you bought, used and have left, with a red
  reorder flag before you run out mid-commission
• 📦 Made & Stocked — batches made, reserved, taken, sold, returned,
  damaged, current and still available
• 🏪 Craft Fairs — one row per market: booth, travel, parking, food and
  display costs, takings, net profit, margin and that fair's best seller
• 💰 Sales Log — every sale with event + product dropdowns, discounts,
  payment method and live cost of sale
• 🧮 Event Profit — pick a fair: revenue, every cost, net profit, margin,
  average sale, break-even and ROI
• 🔄 Reorder List — low products *and* yarn pulled in automatically with
  priority, suggested quantity and estimated budget
• 🎒 Packing Checklist — four packing zones per fair with ticks and a
  % packed meter
• 💵 Pricing Calculator — materials + packaging + your wage + overhead +
  margin → suggested price and a charm price
• 📅 Monthly Summary — the whole year month by month, best month flagged
• 📖 Start Here — 5-minute setup tour, tab guide and 10 pro tips

**BASIC EDITION** keeps the core selling loop (Dashboard, Catalog, Fairs,
Sales Log, Pricing Calculator, Start Here) at a friendlier price.

**TWO THEMES** — Berry Bramble (plum & rose, Georgia serif) and Mint Meadow
(teal & coral, clean sans). Pick the one that feels like your stall.

**EXAMPLE FILES INCLUDED** — every purchase includes a fully filled-in
EXAMPLE workbook (fictional studio "Willow & Wren") so you can see exactly how
it works before touching your own copy.

**USE IT YEAR AFTER YEAR** — the report year is a setting, not hard-coded.
Change it in January and the same file keeps working. Reuse it for Christmas
markets, summer fetes, school fairs and online batches alike.

**WORKS IN** Excel 2016+ (Windows & Mac), Excel for Microsoft 365, and Google
Sheets (import). Formulas only — no macros, no add-ins, nothing to install.

### HOW IT WORKS
1. Open ⚙️ Lists & Settings: type your studio name, currency, wage, overhead
   and margin; rename any dropdown list to match your world.
2. Add your designs to the Product Catalog and your yarn to Materials.
3. Before each fair: check the Reorder List and print the Packing Checklist.
4. At the fair: log sales on your phone (Sheets) or after teardown.
5. After the fair: open Event Profit and the Dashboard — know your numbers
   before the tea goes cold.

## FAQ block

**Will it work in Google Sheets?** Yes — import the .xlsx; formulas, dropdowns
and charts carry over. A couple of print settings may want a tweak.

**Can I add my own products/fairs?** Yes. The tables grow to 24 products,
24 materials, 24 fairs and 60 sales lines per year; the hidden engine expands
automatically.

**Is my data safe?** The file never phones home — it's a plain workbook on
your machine or Drive.

**Do I need Excel skills?** If you can fill a table, you can run this. Every
calculated cell is locked and colour-coded so you always know what's yours to
type.
